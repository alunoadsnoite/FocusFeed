FocusFeed helps you focus and turn completed work into earned breaks.

Choose installed apps to block, start a configurable Pomodoro, and earn 20% of completed focus time as a break balance. Spend that balance to temporarily release selected apps. Blocking resumes automatically when the break ends.

Features:
• Configurable focus timer, pause, resume and stop
• Installed-app selection with icons and search
• Native foreground monitor and blocking overlay
• Local balance and daily completed-focus history
• Session restoration and protection-status feedback
• Completion and break-expiry notifications

No account, advertisements, analytics or backend. All progress stays on your device.

Usage access and draw-over-other-apps access are needed for blocking. Notification permission is optional. Accessibility access is not required.

After a reboot or interruption, unfinished focus is restored paused without awarding credit for downtime. Android may restrict or stop background monitoring, and certain apps can hide overlays. This is a voluntary focus aid, not an unbreakable parental-control system.
